# 05 · Finance — who pays how much for what

Setting: western UP, funding by government in exchange for reach into low-connectivity villages, pest-surveillance data, a farmer registry and local jobs.

| Item | Who pays | How much |
|---|---|---|
| Farmer's call | Nobody | ₹0 (missed call + call-back) |
| Call minutes | UP State Agriculture Department | ~₹11–32 per farmer per year |
| Hardware (mini PC, gateway, battery) | Central government (e.g. IndiaAI Mission / Ministry of Agriculture) | [₹__ one-time per site] |
| Site and electricity | FPO or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (government-funded) | ~300–550 kWh/year × [UP tariff] |
| Digital champion | State, via FPO/local extension | [₹__ per month] |
| Advice content and review | Agricultural universities / ICAR experts | [₹__ per season] |
| Native-speaker recordings | Pilot budget | [₹__ one-time] |
| Officer call-backs | State extension service | existing staff time |
| Integration with Bharat-VISTAAR | Ministry of Agriculture | [₹__ one-time] |
| Anonymised outbreak maps (optional) | Buyers/agri-businesses | [₹__ per season] — revenue, not cost |

## Call cost calculation
- Indian outbound rates found: ~₹0.40–1.20 per minute.
- Advice call ~3.5 min → ~₹1.40–4.20; follow-up ~₹0.40–1.20.
- 6 advice + 6 follow-ups per farmer per year → ~₹11–32 (~$0.13–0.40).
- 1,000 farmers → ~₹11,000–32,000 per year.
- Check billing pulse: per-minute billing rounds short calls up.

## Benchmarks
- PxD: voice advisory at ~$2–5 per farmer per year; costs fell from >$10 (2012–13) to ~$3.41 (2019), marginal ~$0.60.
- SMS advisory study: ~$0.30 per farmer cost, ~$54 average income gain.
- Gujarat cotton farmers stated willingness to pay $2 for a nine-month subscription.
- Limitation: GiveWell rated PxD's likely income effect as small and below its funding bar.

## Jobs created (summit focus)
Native-speaker voice recorders, agronomist content reviewers, one paid digital champion per site.

## Data ownership
Data belongs to the farmers' organisation / public system; shared only aggregated (village level, small counts hidden) and never for price negotiations.

## Pitch line
"Free for the farmer, funded by the state, about ₹11–32 per farmer per year in call costs, extending Bharat-VISTAAR to villages the cloud can't reach."
